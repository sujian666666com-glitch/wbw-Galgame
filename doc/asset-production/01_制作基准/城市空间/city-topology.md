## 提示词

> Create a clean spatial topology concept for Sakurakagami Town, a compact coastal Japanese-inspired fictional town for a schedule-exploration visual novel. Use a readable elevated three-quarter map sketch, not a satellite map. Show six public nodes with believable road and walking relationships: the old-house district and old street as the player's home anchor, Sakurakagami Academy on slightly higher ground, Spring Garden between the academy and old street, the public archive near the academy's northern side, the coast and lighthouse along the sea, and the old railway station at the town boundary. Reserve a concealed underground relationship beneath the station for the late-game Mirror Chamber, but do not visibly reveal the chamber. Keep travel distances compact and intuitive, use the supplied scene images only to preserve architectural identity, and leave room around every landmark for future clickable labels. Neutral daylight, no characters, no event markers, no readable text, no UI frame, no watermark.
